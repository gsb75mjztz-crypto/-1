    Why this order?

    Why budget before saving?

    You can't direct money you can't see. A quick picture of income and spending is what makes every later route possible — otherwise savings and debt targets are just guesses.

    Why an emergency fund before extra debt payments?

    Without a cushion, the next surprise bill becomes new debt. A small starter fund breaks that cycle before you accelerate payoff.

    Why debt before investing?

    High-interest debt is a guaranteed negative return. Clearing it first (after capturing any employer match) usually beats uncertain market gains.
